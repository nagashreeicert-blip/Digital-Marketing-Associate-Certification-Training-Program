Digital marketing is the practice of promoting products or services using online channels and technology.

# ✔️ You will learn:

What is Digital Marketing?

Difference between Digital Marketing, Traditional Marketing & Integrated Marketing

Role of Digital Marketing in business growth

Core disciplines: SEO, SEM, Social Media, Email, Content, Paid Ads

Examples: eCommerce campaigns, lead generation, brand awareness

Always define your audience, campaign goal, and channel strategy before launching marketing efforts.

# 🟦 3. SEO (Search Engine Optimization)

SEO helps your website rank higher on search engines to attract organic traffic.

✔️ Topics covered:

On-Page SEO: meta tags, headings, content optimization

Off-Page SEO: backlinks, influencer marketing, social signals

Technical SEO: site speed, mobile optimization, schema markup

Keyword research & analysis

SEO audit tools (Google Analytics, SEMrush, Ahrefs)

💡 Tip:
Use data-driven keyword strategies and regularly audit your website for SEO improvements.

🟦 4. SEM & Paid Marketing

Learn to run paid campaigns to drive traffic, leads, and conversions.

✔️ You will learn:

Google Ads: search, display, video campaigns

Social Media Ads: Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok

Bid strategies, targeting, and audience segmentation

Measuring ROI & campaign KPIs

💡 Tip:
Start with small budgets, test creatives, and optimize based on performance data.

Paid social advertising

Analytics and engagement tracking
