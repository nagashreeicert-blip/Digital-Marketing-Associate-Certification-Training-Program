# 📘 Digital Marketing Certification Exam Pattern

This guide provides a clear overview of the Digital Marketing certification exam structure to help you prepare effectively.

# ✅ 1. Total Questions
100–120 questions (depending on the exam provider)
All questions are scored

# ✅ 2. Question Types

Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

Scenario-based / situational questions

Some questions may require selecting multiple correct answers

# ✅ 3. Exam Duration
90–120 minutes (1.5–2 hours)
No scheduled breaks

# ✅ 4. Exam Format

Computer-Based Exam (CBE)

Can be taken:

Online (Proctored)

At authorized test centers

# ✅ 5. Domain-wise Weightage

Domain	Weight
SEO & SEM	30%
Social Media & Content Marketing	25%
Email Marketing & Automation	15%
Analytics & Reporting	20%
Paid Campaigns & Strategy	10%

# ✅ 6. Difficulty Level
Moderate difficulty with scenario-based questions
Tests your understanding of digital marketing fundamentals and ability to apply strategies in real-world scenarios

# ✅ 7. Passing Score
Certification providers typically require 65–70% correct answers to pass
Recommended: aim for 75%+ to ensure strong performance

# ✅ 8. Exam Retake Policy

2–3 attempts allowed within 6–12 months of registration

Retake fees may apply
